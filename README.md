## Hi, I'm Chaima Gabsi

Software engineer in Stuttgart, Germany. I build real-time AI and full-stack applications, currently in digital health.

### Now

Engineering thesis at **ACTIMI GmbH** in Stuttgart, on **EmpAItica**: an AI application that helps detect depression early and follow patients between consultations. The patient talks with a 3D avatar, answers the PHQ-8 questionnaire by voice, and the clinician sees the scores and the trend in a dedicated portal.

The parts I work on:

- **Emotion from three channels.** Face analysis on the device with MediaPipe and ONNX Runtime, a bilingual English and German word lexicon, and a voice tone model (emotion2vec+) served by a FastAPI service.
- **Signal stability.** Smoothing and fusion of the three readings, so the emotion shown to the patient stays steady instead of flickering.
- **Real-time conversation.** Voice-to-voice dialogue over WebSocket with a 3D avatar built in three.js.
- **Clinical data.** HL7 FHIR R4 resources (Patient, QuestionnaireResponse, Observation, Consent, Appointment) and a crisis path that no setting can turn off.
- **Apps around it.** React web client, iOS app with Expo and React Native distributed through TestFlight, clinician portal with online video consultations, administration, and a marketing site in six languages.
- **Privacy by design.** Face analysis stays on the device. No camera image is sent to the server.

The code belongs to ACTIMI and stays private.
